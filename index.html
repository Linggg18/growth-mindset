🌱 What is a Growth Mindset?

A Growth Mindset is the belief that your abilities and intelligence can be developed through effort, learning, and persistence. People with a growth mindset don’t fear failure—they see it as a learning opportunity.

This term was introduced by psychologist Carol Dweck, who showed that people who believe they can improve tend to reach higher goals and become more resilient.

So, let’s build a mindset that loves learning, embraces challenges, and grows stronger every day!
